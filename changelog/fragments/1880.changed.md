- `ConstraintsHistory`, `ParallelCoordinates` and `VariableInfluence` draw their reference lines in the color of the axes, given by the matplotlib rcParam `axes.edgecolor`, instead of black.
- `Boxplot` draws its grid in the color given by the matplotlib rcParam `grid.color`, instead of light grey.
- `OptHistoryView` marks the optimal iteration with a star on the x-axis and a dashed line, both in the color of the axes and mentioned in the label of the x-axis, instead of a red line and a legend; red is the color of a violated constraint in these figures.
- `OptHistoryView` marks the iterations whose values are `NaN` with a cross on the x-axis and a dashed line, both in the color of the axes and mentioned in the label of the x-axis, instead of purple lines that nothing documented.
