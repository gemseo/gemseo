`DesignSpace` handles a new type of design variable, `"discrete"`: a scalar variable whose domain is a finite list of numeric values, e.g. $[1, 4, 6, 9]$, instead of an interval. Its bounds are derived from that list, the smallest and the largest value, and cannot be set; a value lying between them but absent from the list is rejected. `DesignSpace.variables` reads the list back, as `design_space.variables[name].choices`, and `DesignSpace.variables.has_discrete_variables` tells whether a design space holds such a variable. `DesignVariableType` has a new `DISCRETE` member.

`DesignSpace.add_variable()` gains a `variable` argument that, when passed, is used as is in place of the size/type/bounds arguments, e.g. `add_variable(name, variable=variable)` or `add_variable(name, variable=variable, value=value)`; this is the only way to declare a variable whose domain is not defined from bounds.

The classes of the variables are public, in the `gemseo.space.variable` package: `BaseVariable`, `ContinuousVariable`, `IntegerVariable` and `DiscreteVariable`.
