- `SobolAnalysis.compute_samples` logs a warning instead of silently replacing the explicit `eval_second_order` setting of `OT_SOBOL_INDICES_Settings` by `compute_second_order` when they differ.
- The `compute_samples` method of the sensitivity analyses and `MorrisDOE` log a warning when they replace the `n_samples` setting set explicitly in the settings of the DOE algorithm, e.g. `SobolAnalysis` by its `n_samples` argument and `MorrisAnalysis` by its `n_replicates` argument.
- The `compute_samples` method of the sensitivity analyses no longer modifies the settings of the DOE algorithm passed as `algo_settings`, e.g. their `n_samples`, and `eval_second_order` for `SobolAnalysis`.
- `MorrisDOE` no longer modifies the `n_samples` of the `doe_algo_settings` of its settings.
- `RandomSpace.untransform_vect` raises a `ValueError` instead of returning infinite values (SciPy) or arbitrary finite values (OpenTURNS) when a component of the point of the unit hypercube is 0 (resp. 1) for a random variable unbounded below (resp. above), e.g. with a DOE algorithm sampling the boundary of the unit hypercube such as an unscrambled SciPy Sobol' sequence.
