- The `threshold` argument of `DisciplineJacApprox.check_jacobian` and `error_max` argument of `ODEProblem.check_jacobian`
  have been replaced by separate `atol` (absolute tolerance) and `rtol` (relative tolerance) arguments,
  both defaulting to `1e-8`.
- The gradient approximator modules have been moved into the new `gemseo.utils.derivatives.approximators` subpackage:
    - `gemseo.utils.derivatives.base_gradient_approximator` → `gemseo.utils.derivatives.approximators.base`
    - `gemseo.utils.derivatives.centered_differences` → `gemseo.utils.derivatives.approximators.centered_differences`
    - `gemseo.utils.derivatives.complex_step` → `gemseo.utils.derivatives.approximators.complex_step`
    - `gemseo.utils.derivatives.factory` → `gemseo.utils.derivatives.approximators.factory`
    - `gemseo.utils.derivatives.finite_differences` → `gemseo.utils.derivatives.approximators.forward_differences`
- The class `FirstOrderFD` was renamed to `ForwardDifferences`.
- The `CenteredDifferences` and `ForwardDifferences` approximators now share a common base class
  `gemseo.utils.derivatives.approximators.base_finite_differences.BaseFiniteDifferences`,
  which holds the finite-difference-specific `compute_optimal_step` method.
