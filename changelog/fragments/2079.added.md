- The new dataset plot `Treemap` (in `gemseo.post.dataset`, configured by `Treemap_Settings` from `gemseo.post.dataset.treemap_settings`) draws one tile per variable of a single-sample dataset in a unit square, using a squarified layout.
- The new method `SobolAnalysis.plot_variance_decomposition` (also available for `ROSobolAnalysis`) plots a treemap showing how the variance of an output is shared out between the first-order and second-order Sobol' indices, with a residual tile for the interactions of higher order.
