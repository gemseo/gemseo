The uncertain variables of a `ParameterSpace` were of type `"float"`, whereas the random variables of a `RandomSpace` are of type `VariableType.RANDOM`, so a test such as `space.variables[name].type == VariableType.REAL`, migrated from `space.get_type(name) == "float"`, is `False` for a random variable; compare to `VariableType.RANDOM` instead.
