Setting `HDF5Cache.file_kept_open` to `True` keeps the HDF file open
between two operations, which is about twice faster,
and `HDF5Cache.close_file()` closes it.
The worker processes of a parallel execution, e.g. of a DOE, do not keep it open,
as HDF5 forbids a process to open a file that another one has open.
An HDF file left open is closed at the end of the process,
whether or not it raises an exception.
