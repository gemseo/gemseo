BREAKING CHANGES:

- The `add_iteration_callback` and `clear_iteration_callbacks` methods of `BaseMDASolver` were renamed to `add_iteration_listener` and `clear_iteration_listeners`, and the `iteration_callback` argument of `add_iteration_callback` was renamed to `iteration_listener`. A callable registered on an object and removable is now called a *listener* everywhere, whereas a *callback* is a callable passed as an argument for a single execution, e.g. the `callbacks` setting of the DOE algorithms.
