The members of all the enumerations now follow the upper-case convention; only the member *names* changed, their *values* are unchanged (e.g. `ColormapName.cool` became `ColormapName.COOL` but still equals `"cool"`, and `DatasetClassName.IODataset` became `DatasetClassName.IO_DATASET` but still equals `"IODataset"`). This affects `Strength`, `Alpha`, `Face`, `Criterion`, `Hypersphere`, `ColormapName`, `ProgressBarDataName`, `DatasetClassName`, the OpenTURNS `DistributionName`/`FittingCriterion`/`SignificanceTest` and the SciPy `DistributionName` enumerations. The old member names keep resolving, with a `DeprecationWarning`, both as attributes and by name, e.g. `ColormapName["cool"]`, except the attribute `Criterion.center`, which is now the method `str.center`: use `Criterion.CENTER` or `Criterion["center"]` instead.
