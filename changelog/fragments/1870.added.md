`OTMaximumEntropyDistribution` and `SPMaximumEntropyDistribution` create the probability distribution maximizing the Shannon entropy for what an expert knows about a random variable, namely a minimum, a maximum, a mean `mu` and a standard deviation `sigma`, instead of a distribution name and its parameters. `MaximumEntropyNumericalSettings` sets the tolerances, the thresholds and the iteration limit of the numerical methods of these distributions.
