- `BaseSensitivityAnalysis.Method` was removed; the method enumeration is now defined outside the class.
- `gemseo.utils.enumeration.merge_enums` and its module were removed; the enumerations previously assembled with it are now defined explicitly.
- The unused `LinearSolver` enumerations of `gemseo.mda.base_settings`, `gemseo.mda.base_solver_settings` and `gemseo.mda.newton_raphson_settings` were removed; the linear solver of an MDA is selected via its `linear_solver_settings` field.
