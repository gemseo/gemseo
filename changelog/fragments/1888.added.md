`EvaluationScenarioAdapter` is a discipline wrapping any `EvaluationScenario`; its outputs are those of the last design point evaluated by the scenario, e.g. the last sample of a DOE algorithm. `MDOScenarioAdapter` derives from it and adds the features requiring an optimum, namely the optimum as outputs, the optimal objective value recorded by the scenario (`output_optimal_objective=True`), the Lagrange multipliers as extra outputs (`output_multipliers=True`) and the post-optimal linearization; it raises a `TypeError` at instantiation when the scenario does not solve an `OptimizationProblem`, and a `ValueError` when the optimal objective value is required while the objective is already known to be multi-valued. This single-valued objective requirement, which the post-optimal linearization shares, is now checked on the dimension of the objective.
