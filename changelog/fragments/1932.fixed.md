- `DIFFERENTIAL_EVOLUTION` and `SHGO` with `workers` other than 1
  no longer return the starting point as the optimum:
  the evaluations of the worker processes are now stored in the database
  and count towards the termination criteria, `max_iter` included.
- `CallableParallelExecution` no longer fails on Windows machines with more than 61 CPUs:
  it now caps the number of processes at 61.
