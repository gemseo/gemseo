- `gemseo.check_jacobian()` checks the analytical Jacobian of a `Discipline` against a numerical approximation; it is the high-level entry point wrapping `DisciplineJacobianChecker`.
- `gemseo.utils.derivatives.check.base.BaseJacobianChecker` is the abstract base class for Jacobian correctness checkers.
- `gemseo.utils.derivatives.check.function.FunctionJacobianChecker` checks the Jacobian of an `ArrayFunction`.
- `gemseo.utils.derivatives.check.discipline.DisciplineJacobianChecker` checks the Jacobian of a `Discipline`.
- `gemseo.utils.derivatives.check.mda.MDAJacobianChecker` checks the Jacobian of a `BaseMDA`.
- `gemseo.utils.derivatives.derivatives_approx.compare_jacobian_matrices()` compares two Jacobian matrices using absolute and relative tolerance parameters.
- The `gemseo.utils.derivatives.approximators` subpackage groups all gradient approximator classes: `BaseGradientApproximator`, `CenteredDifferences`, `ComplexStep`, `ForwardDifferences`, and `GradientApproximatorFactory`.
