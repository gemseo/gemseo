BREAKING CHANGES:

- The module `gemseo.disciplines.scenario_adapters.mdo_scenario_adapter` is now `gemseo.scenario.adapter.mdo`.
- The arguments of the scenario adapters no longer refer to an optimization: `reset_x0_before_opt`, `set_x0_before_opt` and `set_bounds_before_opt` were renamed to `reset_x0_before_exec`, `set_x0_before_exec` and `set_bounds_before_exec`, while `keep_opt_history`, `save_opt_history` and `opt_history_file_prefix` were renamed to `keep_databases`, `save_databases` and `database_file_prefix`. The attributes `keep_opt_history` and `save_opt_history` were renamed accordingly. The settings of `BiLevel` keep their names.
