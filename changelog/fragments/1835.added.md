`ISFORMSobolAnalysis` is a reliability-oriented sensitivity analysis
estimating the Sobol' indices of an event, e.g. a disciplinary output
exceeding a threshold, seen as a failure indicator.
It combines the first-order reliability method (FORM) to locate the design point,
an importance sampling auxiliary density centered on this design point,
and a Sobol' analysis of the importance-sampling-reweighted indicator.
