The public enumerations of GEMSEO appearing in public method/function argument annotations or settings model field annotations can now be imported from the `gemseo.enums` package, e.g. `from gemseo.enums import LinearizationMode`, without knowing where each enumeration is defined. Enumerations whose name is used by two different enum classes (e.g. `CovarianceModel`) are exported under a qualified alias (e.g. `OTGPRCovarianceModel` and `HSICCovarianceModel`).
