`BaseStatistics.compute_interquartile_range()` and `BaseStatistics.compute_quartile_coefficient_of_dispersion()`
compute the interquartile range (symbol `IQR`) and the quartile coefficient of dispersion (symbol `QCD`),
quantile-based and outlier-robust counterparts of the standard deviation and of the coefficient of variation,
for empirical and parametric statistics;
the quartile coefficient of dispersion is meant for data whose first and third quartiles share the same sign.
