- The `updating` setting of `DIFFERENTIAL_EVOLUTION` is now a `DifferentialEvolutionUpdating`,
  available from `gemseo.enum`; its values `"immediate"` and `"deferred"` are still accepted,
  another value now raises a pydantic `ValidationError`.
- The default value of the `updating` setting of `DIFFERENTIAL_EVOLUTION` is now `None`,
  which means `"immediate"` when `workers` is 1 and `"deferred"` otherwise;
  `updating="immediate"` with `workers` other than 1 now raises a pydantic `ValidationError`
  instead of being replaced by `"deferred"` by SciPy.
- The `workers` setting of `DIFFERENTIAL_EVOLUTION` must be a positive integer or -1;
  another value now raises a pydantic `ValidationError` when the settings are created.
- The `workers` setting of `SHGO` now accepts -1 to use all the CPUs.
- With constraints, `DIFFERENTIAL_EVOLUTION` now ignores `workers` and logs a warning,
  as SciPy evaluates the constraints in the calling process.
- The `workers` setting of `DIFFERENTIAL_EVOLUTION` and `SHGO` cannot be used with `use_database=False`;
  doing so now raises a pydantic `ValidationError`.
