- `OTDistribution` no longer rejects valid truncation bounds when the distribution is both transformed and truncated.
- `OTDistribution` no longer reports the numerical range of a transformed distribution as its support where the transformation diverges: such an end is now infinite, e.g. the upper end of an exponential distribution transformed by `exp(x)` is now infinite instead of `1e14`, and so is the lower end of a uniform distribution on `[0, 1]` transformed by `log(x)`, which diverges at 0. On a side that is unbounded before transformation, the numerical range that OpenTURNS estimates is otherwise kept, as before, but is no longer used to reject a truncation bound, even when the transformation has no limit, e.g. `sin(x)`, or is unknown to sympy, e.g. `besselJ0(x)`.
- `OTDistribution.transformation` no longer substitutes the name of the random variable inside the name of a function including it, e.g. the transformation `"exp(x)"` was reported as `e(x)p((x))`.
