- BREAKING CHANGES for enumerations:
    - The `Method` attribute of the sensitivity analyses was extracted:
      `CorrelationAnalysis.Method` was renamed to `CorrelationAnalysisMethod`,
      `HSICAnalysis.Method` to `HSICAnalysisMethod`,
      `MorrisAnalysis.Method` to `MorrisAnalysisMethod`
      and `SobolAnalysis.Method` to `SobolAnalysisMethod`.
    - The hybrid modes were removed from `Discipline.ApproximationMode`, which now holds only `COMPLEX_STEP`, `FINITE_DIFFERENCES` and `CENTERED_DIFFERENCES`; use the new `Discipline.HybridApproximationMode` instead.
    - `DisciplineChain.LinearizationMode` now also accepts `DIRECT`, `ADJOINT` and the hybrid modes.
    - `DerivationMode` now also defines `FORWARD` in addition to `DIRECT`, `ADJOINT`, `REVERSE` and `AUTO`; `DIRECT`/`ADJOINT` denote the MDA resolution modes and `FORWARD`/`REVERSE` the chain-rule accumulation modes. The MDA-specific subset is the new `MDADerivationMode` (in `gemseo.core.derivatives.jacobian_assembly`) and the chain-specific subset is the new `ChainDerivationMode` (in `gemseo.core.chains.chain`); both are importable from `gemseo.enums`.
    - `Discipline.LinearizationMode` no longer exposes the `DerivationMode` members (`DIRECT`, `ADJOINT`, `REVERSE`); a discipline either computes its analytical Jacobian (`AUTO`) or approximates it. It now holds only `AUTO` and the approximation modes.
    - The unused member `SciPyDOE.Optimizer.NONE` was removed.
    - The `EvaluationProblem.DifferentiationMethod.USER_GRAD` member was renamed to `USER`; its value is unchanged.
