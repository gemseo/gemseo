- `Catalog.compute_weighted_properties()` computes the weighted sum of the numerical properties of the alternatives of a catalog, by default normalized into their barycenter, and optionally its Jacobian.
- `Catalog.numerical_property_names` gives the names of the numerical properties of a catalog, in the order `compute_weighted_properties()` weights them.
- `DisciplineAdapterGenerator` and `DisciplineAdapter` give a discipline the numerical properties of the catalog of a `CatalogVariable` input, at the alternative chosen in the design vector.
