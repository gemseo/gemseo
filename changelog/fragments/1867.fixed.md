`ParameterSpace.normalize_vect()` and `ParameterSpace.denormalize_vect()` now take the `minus_lb` argument into account. As a consequence, `ParameterSpace.normalize_grad()` and `ParameterSpace.denormalize_grad()` no longer offset the gradient by the lower bounds. These two methods are used to convert the Jacobians of the functions of an `EvaluationProblem` whose design space is normalized, and the converted Jacobians are stored in the `Database`; consequently, the derivatives computed over a `ParameterSpace` were wrong and gradient-based results may change.
